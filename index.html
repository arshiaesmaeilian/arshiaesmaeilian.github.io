About

I’ve cared for patients in hurricane shelters, screened marine and fungal extracts for new antifungal and anti-tubercular leads, and taught organic chemistry to students convinced they’d never get it. The common thread is helping people through hard moments with careful, clear work.

Raised in Dubai as an Iranian national, I’ve never lived in a country that offered me a permanent home. That experience shaped how I see the barriers immigrants face, and it drove my work leading USF’s Iranian Students Association and speaking to the press on behalf of Iranian and international students. It’s also why I’m committed to health equity for immigrant communities.

I’m now looking for research, clinical research, and healthcare roles in New York, where I can put my chemistry and patient care experience to work.

Projects and presentations

Laboratory research · Aug 2024 to May 2025

Candida Crushers: Discovering Novel Antifungal Metabolites from Endophytic Fungi via a Bioassay-Guided Approach

Resistance is rising among Candida species, including Candida auris. We studied TAP14-269D-4, an endophytic Talaromyces species isolated from pine tree roots in Tapachula, Mexico, for its potent activity against C. albicans and C. auris. Using a bioassay-guided approach, we combined HRMS, NMR spectroscopy, and AI-assisted de-replication tools such as SMART NMR and DeepSAT to identify and characterize its secondary metabolites.

Presented at the Harvard Undergraduate Research Conference (HURC), FURC, and USF Castle

Literature review · Sep 2023 to Apr 2024

Alcohol’s Long-Term Effects on Cardiomyocytes and Development of Alcoholic Cardiomyopathy

Alcoholic dilated cardiomyopathy is linked to chronic alcohol abuse and marked by altered mRNA expression and structural changes in the heart that can lead to heart failure. We reviewed decades of human and animal studies on the roles of alcohol, genetic predisposition, and comorbidities. The review highlights acetaldehyde’s part in cardiac remodeling, and abstinence or moderation as the most effective treatment.

Presented at OneUSF

Research in French · Apr 2025

Enseignant contre machine : la différence de rétention linguistique à long terme entre les utilisateurs d’applications d’apprentissage des langues et les élèves des classes traditionnelles

Teacher versus machine. Apps like Duolingo, Babbel, and Busuu have made language learning more accessible, convenient, and affordable. This study, written and presented in French, asks whether they are actually more effective than in-person teaching for long-term language retention.
